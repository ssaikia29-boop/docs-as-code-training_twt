## Getting started   

### 2.1 Logging in   

1. Open the LearnSphere portal.   
2. Enter your username and password.   
3. Select **Sign In**.   

![Learnsphere login page](./Image.md/LearnSphere%20LMS%20Login%20Page.png)

>**Note**
Contact your system administrator if you do not have login credentials.
