## 12. Frequently Asked Questions   

### 12.1 Can I delete a project?   

Only project owners and administrators can delete projects.   

### 12.2 Can I change the assignee of a task?   

Yes. Open the task, select a different user from the Assignee list, and save your changes.   

### 12.3 Can I restore a deleted task?

Deleted tasks can be restored by administrators within 30 days.   

### 12.4 Can I access TaskFlow from a mobile device?   

Yes. TaskFlow can be accessed through supported mobile browsers.
